# Decision Framework

## Core principle

Optimize **value**, not simply the lowest observed compensation.

### Step 1 — Demand
Use job-posting volume as a market-demand signal.

### Step 2 — Economics
Normalize observed compensation and currency effects.

### Step 3 — Feasibility
Identify missing internal capacity/client constraints instead of inventing them.

### Step 4 — Scenario
Use explicit user-defined assumptions for incremental demand, FX shocks or wage changes.

### Step 5 — Decision
Recommend a shortlist only when the public evidence and stated assumptions support it.
